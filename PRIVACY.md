# Privacy Policy for Moesalaat

**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026  

Moesalaat ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and information handling.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
- **No Personal Data Collection:** Moesalaat does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, IP address, or device identifiers.
- **Local Data Only:** All application settings, prayer tracking habits, preferences, and reading progress are stored strictly on your local device (via your browser's local storage within the desktop sandbox).
- **Location Data:** If location-based prayer calculation is used, your coordinates are processed strictly locally on your computer to calculate astronomical prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and are never uploaded to any external server.

---

### 2. Permissions and Capabilities
- **Full Trust (`runFullTrust`):** Moesalaat operates as a native Windows desktop utility. This capability allows the application to:
  - Play local audio notifications when it is time for Adhan.
  - Interact with the Windows native notification center.
  - Run efficiently in the system tray when minimized.
  - Store your local prayer tracker data offline on your machine.
- No network requests containing user telemetry or tracking are executed by the application.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
Moesalaat does not integrate third-party analytics, advertisements, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

---

### 4. Security
Because all data remains on your personal computer, the security of your data is governed by your operating system and device security controls.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
